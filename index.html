Meet the founder

“I want to help dentists bridge the gap between what they learn and what modern clinical practice demands.”

Dr. Tanushi Uniyal graduated from Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences in Kochi, India in 2008 and completed her DDS at the University of Alberta, Canada in 2019. She has practiced dentistry in the United States for the past three years.

For approximately four years, she also mentored internationally trained dentists in association with the Alberta Network of Immigrant Women (ANIW).
